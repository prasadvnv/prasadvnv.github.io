About

From mechanical design to immersive systems

I come from mechanical engineering and am completing an M.Eng. in Mechatronics and Cyber Physical Systems at Deggendorf Institute of Technology.

Most of my software work is Unity and C# for XR: six months as an XR developer intern at Lufthansa Technik, and now the XR2ACH vocational training project at the Virtual Dimension Center. Alongside that I have academic experience in robotics simulation with ROS and Gazebo, and in engineering design with MATLAB, Simulink and SolidWorks.

The common thread is systems where software has to deal with physical things: trackers, headsets, sensors, robots, and the people using them.
